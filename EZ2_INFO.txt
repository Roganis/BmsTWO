EZ2 info fields (judgement_deltas / life_deltas)
=================================================

These two objects live in "info" and map onto the original game's per-chart
song .ini: judgement_deltas is the [JudgmentDelta] section and life_deltas is
the [GaugeUpDownRate] section. BmsTWO round-trips them as extra fields; the
EZ2 converter reads them. EZ2 modes ignore bmson's judge_rank and total.


judgement_deltas - UNIT IS CHART TICKS, NOT MILLISECONDS
--------------------------------------------------------

	1 tick = 1/192 beat = 312.5 / BPM ms   (2.08 ms at 150 BPM)

Windows are beat-relative: the same numbers are tighter in absolute time on
a faster chart. Each value is the half-width of the window around the note;
MISS is the outer bound, a press further out than that consumes no note.

Store the raw file values. The game never judges with them directly: at
chart setup it adds +3 ticks to all four (+1 in the alternate scoring mode)
before loading them into the judge. Do not add the 3 yourself.

	Original defaults (missing .ini):

		KOOL = 6      judged as  9
		COOL = 24     judged as 27
		GOOD = 36     judged as 39
		MISS = 72     judged as 75

	Effective widths with the +3 widening, default values:

		BPM   tick ms   KOOL     COOL     GOOD      MISS
		120   2.60      +-23 ms  +-70 ms  +-102 ms  +-195 ms
		150   2.08      +-19 ms  +-56 ms  +-81 ms   +-156 ms
		180   1.74      +-16 ms  +-47 ms  +-68 ms   +-130 ms
		200   1.56      +-14 ms  +-42 ms  +-61 ms   +-117 ms

	Guidance (ticks), shipped charts commonly override GOOD=50 / MISS=70:

		KOOL = 4 to 7
		COOL = 15 to 30
		GOOD = 36 to 60
		MISS = 50 to 80


life_deltas - gauge change per judgement
----------------------------------------

There is no KOOL entry: the game mirrors COOL into KOOL. The 14-key modes
add +0.05 to COOL and +0.02 to GOOD on load, and EZ2CATCH zeroes GOOD; both
are applied by the game per mode, so do not bake them into the chart.

	Original defaults (missing .ini):

		COOL = 0.2
		GOOD = 0.1
		MISS = -1.8
		FAIL = -4.8

	Guidance:

		COOL = 0.2 to 0.3
		GOOD = 0.1 to 0.2
		MISS = -1.5 to -2.0
		FAIL = -3.5 to -5.0


Examples
--------

	judgement_deltas:

		KOOL = 6,  COOL = 24, GOOD = 36, MISS = 72   (defaults)
		KOOL = 6,  COOL = 24, GOOD = 50, MISS = 70   (common shipped override)
		KOOL = 7,  COOL = 30, GOOD = 50, MISS = 80   (lenient)

	life_deltas:

		COOL = 0.2, GOOD = 0.1, MISS = -1.8, FAIL = -4.8   (defaults)
		COOL = 0.2, GOOD = 0.1, MISS = -1.5, FAIL = -3.5   (forgiving)
		COOL = 0.3, GOOD = 0.2, MISS = -1.5, FAIL = -4.5   (fast recovery)
